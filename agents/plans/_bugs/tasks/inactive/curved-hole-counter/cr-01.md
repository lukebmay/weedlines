# Curved hole counter

Three defects remain in the change. The named checks passed.
A touch is reported as a self-overlap. A crossing of neighboring
pieces of one figure is not. One older bug is outside this change.

## Defects

1. Neighboring pieces of one subpath are not compared, so a
   crossing away from their joint is not a self-overlap. One open
   subpath is the line (0, 0) to (10, 0), then the cubic
   (10, 0), (10, 5), (0, -5), (0, 2). The cubic crosses that line
   near (0.51, 0) and (4.36, 0). Neither point is the joint.
   `classify_figures` reports only the dangling ends.
   `_piece_hits` finds both crossings. `_self_overlap_defects`
   skips the pair because the pieces are adjacent. The same skip
   drops two cubics that cross, near (0.34, 0.71) and
   (2.86, 0.17): cubic (0, 0), (2, 5), (4, -5), (6, 0), then
   cubic (6, 0), (5, 4), (1, -4), (0, 2).
   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_self_overlap_defects`.
   Remedy: compare adjacent pieces. Keep dropping a hit that is
   only the shared endpoint. A smooth split of one cubic does not
   become a self-overlap, and the four cubics of the radius-10
   ellipse do not either. Add the line-and-cubic case to
   `tests/test_figures.py`. Those two crossings are self-overlaps,
   and the dangling ends stay. The same line followed by the cubic
   (10, 0), (12, 2), (14, 2), (16, 0) only meets at the joint and
   is not a self-overlap.

2. A line that touches a cubic and does not change sides is a
   self-overlap. The open cubic (0, 0), (0, 3), (3, 3), (3, 0)
   peaks at (1.5, 2.25). The line (-1, 2.25) to (4, 2.25) only
   touches there. The figure check reports one self-overlap at
   that point. The old chord test reported none. The same line
   moved to y=2.05 crosses the cubic twice, near (0.85, 2.05) and
   (2.15, 2.05), and those two self-overlaps must stay. A flat
   tangent that does change sides must stay one self-overlap: the
   cubic (0, -0.125), (1/3, 0.125), (2/3, -0.125), (1, 0.125)
   crosses y=0 at (0.5, 0).
   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_line_cubic_hits`.
   Remedy: count a root only when the cubic changes sides of the
   line. Rejecting every root with a flat tangent drops the
   inflection. Add the touch, the secant, and the inflection to
   `tests/test_figures.py`. The open ends stay dangling ends.

3. Two curves that touch and do not cross are two self-overlaps,
   and the reported points are not on the outlines. A radius-10
   circle and a radius-3 circle centered 7 units from the origin
   along y=x meet within 1e-10 at about (7.071, 7.071). Sampled
   points of the inner outline stay in the outer fill except that
   boundary point. The figure check reports self-overlaps near
   (7.073, 7.069) and (7.069, 7.073). The old chord test reported
   none. The chords are crossing about 0.002 away from the touch.
   Flatness 1e-6 does not stop that. A radius-3 circle centered 6
   units out has a gap, is not a self-overlap, and stays the
   counter.
   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_cubic_pair_hits`.
   Remedy: a chord cross is not enough. Count the pair only when
   the curves change sides. Add the touching circles to
   `tests/test_figures.py` and expect no self-overlap. Do not
   require the counter in that test. The hole still is not the
   counter for the reason below.

## Summary

The change fits the reported miss. A radius-10 ellipse and a
triangle in the gap between the curve and a chord are one figure.
The outlines do not cross, so there is no self-overlap. The
counter, the hole of the figure, stays: the figure and the
subject each keep the outer outline and the counter. The triangle
centroid is weed area. The ellipse center is subject. The same
triangle with its vertex at radius 9, inside the chords, is the
same result. That vertex is not in the gap.

The border test no longer uses chord vertices from
`toSubpathPolygons`. A line meets a cubic from the cubic
polynomial. Two cubics are split until each chord is within 1e-6
of its curve, or until depth 32. A cubic that crosses itself is
one self-overlap. The painted close of an open subpath is still
not a border. The piece walk stays in `figures.py`. The surround
module, the window, and the host are not edited. The guide is
not edited.

The code does not fit the self-overlap rule for a touch, or for
a crossing of neighboring pieces. Those are the defects above.

skip is not what ignores the joint. Removing the skip does not
make a self-overlap out of a de Casteljau split of one cubic.
The suite still does not lock a cubic that crosses itself. That
case reports one self-overlap today, at the crossing, including
the open cubic (0, 0), (2, 2), (-1, 2), (1, 0) at (0.5, 0.6).

at the curve joints and is not a self-overlap. The line at y=1
is. The old chord test agreed. That is the existing endpoint
rule, not this defect.
