# Curved hole counter

Two defects remain in the change. The named checks passed.
A straight cubic that crosses another piece of the same figure
is not a self-overlap. The line through the same ends is.

A straight cubic is a curve piece whose control points lie on
the line between its ends.

## Defects

1. A straight cubic with uneven control points crosses another
   piece of the figure and is not a self-overlap. The side test
   treats the fraction along the line between the ends as the
   curve's own parameter. On an unsplit straight cubic those
   are different points, so both samples stay on one side of
   the other piece.

   The open cubic (0, 0), (0, 3), (3, 3), (3, 0) and the
   straight cubic (-1, 2.05), (1, 2.05), (2, 2.05), (4, 2.05)
   cross near (0.849, 2.05) and (2.151, 2.05). The named check
   uses that second piece as a line and reports both
   self-overlaps. As a cubic the figure check reports none.
   The fraction 0.37 along the line is the crossing, but the
   curve parameter 0.37 sits near x=0.91. The curve's own
   parameter for (0.849, 2.05) is about 0.357, and the samples
   there are on opposite sides. The same straight cubic moved
   to y=2.25 does not cross, and it stays no self-overlap.

   One crossing shows the same miss. The open cubic (0, -1),
   (3, -1), (7, 1), (10, 1) crosses the straight cubic (0, 0),
   (0.2, 0), (0.4, 0), (10, 0) at (5, 0). The figure check
   reports no self-overlap. The line through those ends
   reports that point.

   A radius-10 ellipse and the straight cubic (-12, 1),
   (-11.5, 1), (-11, 1), (12, 1) cross near (±9.951, 1). Even
   control points, one third and two thirds of the way from
   (-12, 1) to (12, 1), report those two self-overlaps. The
   uneven control points report none.

   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_cubics_change_sides`.
   Remedy: a flat cubic is a line. Call `_line_cubic_hits`
   with its endpoints and the other curve. Do not sample a
   side at the fraction along the chord. The y=2.05 pair is
   two self-overlaps, and (5, 0) is one. y=2.25 stays none.
   Add the uneven cubic next to the line in
   `tests/test_figures.py`.

2. A straight cubic at a flat inflection is not a self-overlap
   even when the control points are evenly spaced. The
   parameter already matches the crossing. The samples lie on
   opposite sides, about 1e-12 apart, and
   `_curve_changes_side` ignores a change smaller than 1e-9.

   The open cubic (0, -0.125), (1/3, 0.125), (2/3, -0.125),
   (1, 0.125) crosses y=0 at (0.5, 0). The line from
   (-0.2, 0) to (1.2, 0) is the named check and is one
   self-overlap. The straight cubic with control points one
   third and two thirds of the way along that same line is
   none.

   The tangent circles are not this miss. Samples there are
   the same sign, about 1e-6. The hump against y=2.25 is the
   same sign, about 1e-7.

   Deleting the 1e-9 floor does not fix defect 1. Those
   samples are the same sign and about 0.03. Projecting the
   chord point onto the curve does not fix this defect.

   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_curve_changes_side`.
   Remedy: the flat-cubic call in defect 1 also fixes this
   crossing. The straight cubic is flat, and
   `_line_cubic_hits` already keeps the inflection and
   rejects a touch. Add that even straight cubic beside the
   line in `tests/test_figures.py`. It is one self-overlap
   at (0.5, 0).

## Summary

The change fits the reported miss for the outlines the checks
name. A radius-10 ellipse and a triangle in the gap between
the curve and a chord are one figure. The outlines do not
cross, so there is no self-overlap. The counter, the hole of
the figure, stays. The figure and the subject each keep the
outer outline and the counter. The triangle centroid is weed
area. The ellipse center is subject. The triangle at radius 9
is the same result.

Neighboring pieces are compared. The line (0, 0) to (10, 0)
and the cubic (10, 0), (10, 5), (0, -5), (0, 2) are two
self-overlaps, near (0.510, 0) and (4.356, 0). The dangling
ends stay. A cubic that only meets that line at the joint is
not a self-overlap. A shared endpoint is not a crossing. Two
neighboring cubics that cross are one self-overlap. A line at
the peak of the hump, y=2.25, is not a self-overlap. The line
at y=2.05 is two. The flat inflection against that line is
one. The radius-10 circle and the radius-3 circle centered 7
units out along y=x meet within about 1.1e-10 and are not a
self-overlap.

The code does not fit that rule when the straight piece is a
cubic. Defects 1 and 2 are that miss. One change covers both:
a flat cubic uses the line test. Neither defect alone is the
whole fix.

The border pieces are the path's lines and curves.
`_painted_parts` was not changed. The surround module, the
window, the host, and the guide were not edited.
