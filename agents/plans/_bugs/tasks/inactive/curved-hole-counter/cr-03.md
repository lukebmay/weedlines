# Curved hole counter

Three defects remain in the change. The named checks passed.
A straight cubic whose controls lie on the segment between its
ends is that segment. When that segment crosses another piece of
the same figure, the figure check reports a self-overlap. A touch
that stays on one side does not. The crossing is missed again when
the controls leave that segment. The side floor still drops a
crossing of two curves that are not flat.

## Defects

1. An uneven cubic that is not within 1e-9 of the segment between
   its ends is not a self-overlap where that segment crosses.
   `_cubic_pair_hits` calls `_line_cubic_hits` only inside that
   band. Outside it, `_cubics_change_sides` still treats the
   fraction along the chord as the curve's own parameter. On an
   uneven cubic those are different points, so both samples stay
   on one side of the other piece.

   The open cubic (0, 0), (0, 3), (3, 3), (3, 0) and the cubic
   (-1, 2.05), (1, 2.0501), (2, 2.0499), (4, 2.05) cross near
   (0.849, 2.05) and (2.151, 2.05). The figure check reports no
   self-overlap. The same cubic with its controls on y=2.05
   reports both. The same shape at y=2.25, controls 1e-4 off the
   line, stays no self-overlap. A bow of 1e-9 is already outside
   the straight test, and that hump is already none.

   A radius-10 ellipse and the cubic (-12, 1), (-11.5, 1.01),
   (-11, 0.99), (12, 1) cross near (-9.950, 1.001) and
   (9.951, 0.999). The figure check reports only the second.
   Controls on y=1 report both. A bow of 0.1 reports both. The
   samples at the chord fraction are the same sign and about 1
   apart, so the 1e-9 floor is not what drops them.

   Widening the 1e-9 straight test up to the 1e-6 chord tolerance
   does not cover this. The hump at 1e-4 and the ellipse at 0.01
   are both past that tolerance.

   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_cubics_change_sides`.
   Remedy: project the chord point onto each curve. Do not sample
   a side at the fraction along the chord. The hump with controls
   1e-4 off y=2.05 is two self-overlaps. The ellipse and that
   cubic are two. y=2.25 stays none.

2. The 1e-9 floor in `_curve_changes_side` still drops a real
   crossing of two curves that are not flat. The samples have
   opposite signs, and the floor throws the change away.

   The open cubic (0, -0.125), (1/3, 0.125), (2/3, -0.125),
   (1, 0.125) crosses the cubic (0, 0), (1/3, 5e-6),
   (2/3, -5e-6), (1, 0) at (0.5, 0). Neither cubic is straight.
   The second is not within the 1e-6 chord tolerance: its controls
   sit 5e-6 off the segment. The samples are about ±7.51e-10.
   The figure check reports no self-overlap. Controls on the
   segment report the one self-overlap. Controls 1e-5 off the
   segment also report it, because those samples clear 1e-9.

   The tangent circles are not this miss. Their samples are the
   same sign, about 1.7e-6. The hump against y=2.25 is a same-sign
   touch and stays no self-overlap. Removing the floor reports the
   inflection and leaves both of those touches out. The figure
   tests still pass with the floor removed. Removing the floor
   does not fix defect 1. Projecting the chord point does not fix
   this defect.

   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_curve_changes_side`.
   Remedy: an opposite sign is a side change. Do not require the
   samples to exceed 1e-9. A same-sign pair stays out. Add the
   inflection against the cubic whose controls are 5e-6 off the
   segment. It is one self-overlap at (0.5, 0).

3. A cubic whose controls lie on the line through its ends, but
   not on the segment between those ends, runs past that segment.
   Flatness is the perpendicular distance to the line, so this
   cubic looks perfectly flat. `_cubic_pair_walk` replaces it with
   the endpoint chord and does not look at the rest of the curve.
   `_straight_cubic_line` correctly refuses the shortcut, because
   the controls are not on the segment. The walk then misses the
   crossing anyway.

   The open cubic (0, 0), (-1, 0), (2, 0), (1, 0) lies on the
   x-axis and runs from about x=-0.207 to x=1.207. The open cubic
   (0.6, -0.4), (1.4, -0.3), (0.9, 0.2), (1.6, 0.5) crosses it
   at about (1.146, 0). That point is not on the segment from
   (0, 0) to (1, 0). The figure check reports no self-overlap.
   A vertical line at x=1.15 through the first cubic is one
   self-overlap, so the curve does reach past the end. Splitting
   to depth 32 does not find the cubic crossing: the collinear
   piece stays the endpoint chord while the other piece uses the
   depth. `_line_cubic_hits` on the segment between the extreme
   points of the curve, about (-0.207, 0) to (1.207, 0), reports
   (1.146, 0).

   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_cubic_pair_walk`.
   Remedy: a collinear cubic is the segment between the extreme
   points of the curve, not the segment between its ends. Pass
   that segment to `_line_cubic_hits`. Do not use the control box.
   A control can sit outside the curve.

## Summary

The change fits the straight cubics the checks name. A radius-10
ellipse and a triangle in the gap between the curve and a chord
are one figure. The outlines do not cross, so there is no
self-overlap. The counter, the hole of the figure, stays. The
figure and the subject each keep the outer outline and the
counter. The triangle centroid is weed area. The ellipse center
is subject. The triangle at radius 9 is the same result.

Neighboring pieces are compared. The line (0, 0) to (10, 0) and
the cubic (10, 0), (10, 5), (0, -5), (0, 2) are two
self-overlaps. A cubic that only meets that line at the joint is
not a self-overlap. A shared endpoint is not a crossing. A line
at the peak of the hump, y=2.25, is not a self-overlap. The line
at y=2.05 is two. The flat inflection against that line is one.
The radius-10 circle and the radius-3 circle centered 7 units out
along y=x meet within 2e-10 and are not a self-overlap.

The hump and the straight cubic (-1, 2.05), (1, 2.05), (2, 2.05),
(4, 2.05) are two self-overlaps, near (0.849, 2.05) and
(2.151, 2.05). The same straight cubic at y=2.25 is none. The
cubic (0, -1), (3, -1), (7, 1), (10, 1) and the straight cubic
(0, 0), (0.2, 0), (0.4, 0), (10, 0) are one self-overlap at
(5, 0). The inflection against the even straight cubic from
(-0.2, 0) to (1.2, 0) is one, at (0.5, 0). Two straight cubics
that only meet at a corner are not a self-overlap.

The code does not fit the self-overlap rule for the three figures
in the defects. Defects 1 and 2 are the last pass, still present
once the cubic is outside the 1e-9 band. Neither remedy alone is
the whole fix. Defect 3 is the collinear curve that runs past its
ends.

The border pieces are the path's lines and curves.
`_painted_parts` was not changed. The surround module, the
window, the host, and the guide were not edited.
