# Curved hole counter

One defect remains in the change. The named checks passed.
A straight cubic whose controls lie on the segment between its
ends is that segment. A touch that stays on one side is not a
self-overlap. An uneven cubic is sampled on the curve. An
opposite sign is a side change. A collinear cubic whose controls
lie on the line through its ends is the segment between the
extreme points of the curve. The same extra length is missed
when those controls leave the line.

   extra length once the controls leave the 1e-9 band. The
   outlines still cross. The figure check reports no
   self-overlap.

   `_collinear_extent` accepts the cubic only when both controls
   are within 1e-9 of the endpoint line. Past that,
   `_cubic_pair_walk` stops on a chord between ends, or between
   the ends of one split. That chord does not contain the extra
   length. The side test is not what drops the crossing. For a
   bow of 1e-6, 2e-6, or 3e-6 it is never called.

   same cubic with its controls on y=0 is one self-overlap
   there. A bow of 1.1e-9 is already none. A bow of 3e-6 is

   every bow in the gap. A vertical line at x=1.15 also reports

   Widening the 1e-9 gate up to the 1e-6 chord tolerance does

   File: `src/weedlib/figures.py`, `_cubic_pair_walk`.
   Remedy: an endpoint chord stands in for a cubic only when
