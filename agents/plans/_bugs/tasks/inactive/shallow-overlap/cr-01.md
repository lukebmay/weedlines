## Defects

1. A radius-10 circle centered at the origin, and a rectangle,
   are two figures. Before rotation the rectangle runs from
   x=-3 to x=3 and from y=9.99 to y=9.995. It is then rotated
   45 degrees about the origin. The outlines cross. The
   intersection polygon is empty. A point of the rectangle
   near (-9.153, 4.978) lies outside the circle. The origin
   lies outside the rectangle. The figure check reports two
   separate-figure overlaps, near (-7.112, 7.019) and
   (-7.019, 7.112). Both targets lie in both interiors. The
   segment between them lies in both interiors, so this is
   one connected region of shared area.
   `_overlap_defects` in `src/weedlib/figures.py` uses
   `_crossing_overlap_points` when the polygon has no area.
   That helper emits one target for each nearer pair of
   outline crossings. This rectangle has four crossings, and
   the two nearer pairs are the two ends of the same region.

   Remedy: report one separate-figure overlap. The target is
   one point in that region that both interiors contain. A
   nearer pair of outline crossings is not a region.

2. A radius-10 circle centered at the origin is one figure.
   The other figure has two subpaths: a radius-3 circle
   centered at (8, 0), and a radius-3 circle centered about
   (9.171, 9.171), which is 12.97 from the origin along y=x.
   The interiors share two connected regions. The
   intersection polygon keeps only the region with the
   circle at (8, 0). Its area is about 22.59. The point
   about (7.066, 7.066) lies in the outer interior and in
   the circle near (9.171, 9.171), and it does not lie in
   the circle at (8, 0). The center near (9.171, 9.171)
   lies outside the outer interior. The origin lies outside
   both smaller circles. The figure check reports one
   separate-figure overlap, near (7.712, 0). The empty
   polygon of the other region is ignored because the first
   region still has area.

   Remedy: report that second region as a separate-figure
   overlap too. The target is a point in it that both
   interiors contain. A polygon that still has area must
   not drop a connected region the polygon missed.

Both defects are one rule. Each connected region of shared
area is one separate-figure overlap. An empty intersection
polygon does not erase a region. The target is one point in
that region that both interiors contain. The radius-10
circle and the radius-3 circle with centers 7 apart stay
nesting, not a separate-figure overlap.

## Summary

The reported pair is fixed. A radius-10 circle and a
radius-3 circle, centers 12.97 apart along y=x, are two
figures. The outlines cross. The intersection polygon is
empty. The figure check reports one separate-figure
overlap, and the target near (7.066, 7.066) lies in both
interiors. Distance 12.96 still has polygon area and stays
one. Distance 13 stays none. Distance 7 stays nesting.

The new branch does not follow the overlap rule past that
pair. An empty polygon is counted as one overlap per nearer
pair of outline crossings. A polygon that still has area
never looks for a region the polygon missed.

Two separate regions that the polygon drops entirely are
still two overlaps, and that result has to stay. One figure
is two radius-10 circles centered 40 apart on the x-axis.
The other is two radius-3 circles, each centered 12.97
along y=x from one of those centers. The targets are about
(7.066, 7.066) and (47.066, 7.066). The segment between
those targets leaves both interiors.

`tests/test_figures.py`. The nesting test was not changed.

were not edited.
