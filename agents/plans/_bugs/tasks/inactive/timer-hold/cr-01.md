`test_hold_on_a_thread_blocks_the_gui_timer` in
`tests/test_window_timing.py`. The worker waits on an event,
then `_burn_hold(20000000)` runs. The test arms a 0ms timer
and calls `processEvents` until that call both delivers the
timer and takes more than 0.05s. One call can return before
the worker enters the non-yielding loop. Those calls stay
under the threshold and the loop continues.

still fails `assert blocked > 0.05`. The dialog was not
edited. The child-hold check was not edited. It still
records that a 0ms timer fired while the dialog was visible
and the child was alive.
