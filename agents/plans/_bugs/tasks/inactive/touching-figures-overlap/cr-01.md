# cr-01

No defect remains in the change. The named checks passed.
One bug is outside this change. Two figures whose interiors
partially intersect are not reported as a separate-figure
overlap when the intersection path has no area.

## Summary

The change fits the report. Two figures, a radius-10 circle
and a radius-3 circle with centers 7 apart along y=x, meet
within 2e-10. The outlines do not cross. Subtracting the
outer fill from the inner fill leaves a sliver of area
0.003157. The filled point of that sliver, about
(7.054, 7.054), lies in the outer fill, so `_outside_fill`
is false. The outer fill does have area outside the inner
fill.

Exclusive area now uses `_outside_fill` in both directions.
A leftover the other fill contains is not a point outside
it. The outlines do not cross, so the pair is nesting, not
a separate-figure overlap.

A cross of the real outlines still counts.
`_outlines_cross` compares the borders of the two figures,
and it runs only after the intersection has area. From
7.001 through 7.07 the inner `_outside_fill` is false and
the outlines do cross. The figure check reports one
separate-figure overlap there. Using `_outside_fill` alone
would drop that range. At 7.0001 the same split already
holds. By 7.08 the leftover sample itself lies outside the
outer fill, and the overlap stays.

Distance 6 is nesting. Distance 8 is a separate-figure
overlap. An external touch at distance 13 is not an
overlap. Centers 7 apart on the x-axis leave no sliver,
the outlines do not cross, and that pair is nesting.
Centers 6.999 and 6.9999 apart along y=x are nesting too.

The regression is
`test_touching_contained_circles_are_not_a_separate_overlap`.
The touching-hole test was not changed. The product diff
is `src/weedlib/figures.py` and `tests/test_figures.py`.

## Checks

- Distance 7 is not a separate-figure overlap. The outlines
  do not cross, and the sliver's filled point lies in the
  outer fill. Measured on these paths, and covered by the
  new test.
- Distance 6 reports none. Same test.
- Distance 8 is one separate-figure overlap. Same test.
- Distances 7.001 and 7.07 are one separate-figure overlap
  each. The inner `_outside_fill` is false there, and the
  outlines cross, so the cross is what keeps the overlap.
  The new test locks the result. The false helper was
  measured on these paths.
- `pytest tests/test_figures.py` passed: 25 passed. Qt
  loaded. The tests do not load Inkscape.

## Outside this change

Two figures, a radius-10 circle and a radius-3 circle,
centers 12.97 apart along y=x. The outlines cross, twice.
The point about (7.064, 7.064) lies in both fills, and so
do the points about (7.068, 7.068) and (7.070, 7.070).
The inner center, about (9.171, 9.171), lies outside the
outer fill. The origin lies outside the inner fill. The
interiors partially intersect, so this pair is a
separate-figure overlap. The shared interior of the two
true circles has area about 0.0149.

`intersected().simplified()` in `_overlap_defects` in
`src/weedlib/figures.py` has no area. The function returns
before the outline test. The figure check reports no
defect. At distance 12.96 the intersection still has area
and one separate-figure overlap is reported. At distance
13 the outlines do not cross and there is no overlap. The
empty-intersection return was already there before this
change.

Remedy: when the outlines cross and each fill contains a
point of the other, report one separate-figure overlap
even if the intersection has no area. The target is a
point both fills contain, not a point on the outline.
Not fixed in this pass.
