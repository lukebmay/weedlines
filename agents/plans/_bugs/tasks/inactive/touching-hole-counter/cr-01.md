## Summary

The report is confirmed from the paths, not from the note.
A radius-10 circle and a radius-3 circle whose centers are 7
apart along y=x meet within 2e-10. The outlines do not cross.
`inner.subtracted(outer).simplified()` is a triangle of area
0.003157. The interior point of that triangle, about
(7.054, 7.054), lies in the outer fill and in the inner fill.
The vertex on the shared midpoint is not an interior point.
The old area test treated the triangle as area outside, so
the inner circle was not the counter. The inner center stayed
subject. That pair is not a self-overlap. The same circle
centered 6 units out leaves no leftover area and was already
the counter.

The change fits that report. `_outside_fill` still subtracts,
then ignores a leftover whose filled point the outer fill
contains. That leftover is the chord gap of the curve, not
area outside. A point the outer fill does not contain still
blocks the counter. A leftover with no interior point blocks
it too.

The touching hole stays the counter. The inner center is weed
area. The weed area covers the inner fill: those two areas
match, about 25.46, and the subject does not meet that fill.
The figure path keeps both subpaths. The subject is one
contour, because the outlines meet, and that contour still
carries both rings. It has an element at (-10, 0) and an
element at the inner circle's left point. The figure check
reports no self-overlap.

A real crossing is still not a counter. The border test was
not changed. At center distance 7.001 the outlines cross, the
figure check reports two self-overlaps, and the inner center
stays subject. `_outside_fill` is still false there. The
leftover sample sits in the outer fill, and the border test
is what blocks the counter. By distance 7.08 the leftover
sample itself is outside the outer fill. At distance 8 the
cap outside the outer circle is subject. An external touch,
centers 13 apart, is not a counter and is not a self-overlap.

The gapped circle at distance 6 stays the counter. Its center
is weed area, the origin is subject, and the subject keeps
two subpaths.

The surround module, the window, the host, and the guide were
not edited.

## Outside this change

Two figures, not one figure. A radius-10 circle and a
radius-3 circle, centers 7 apart along y=x. Every sampled
point of the inner fill is inside the outer fill: 31397
points, none outside. The outlines do not cross. Nesting is
not a separate-figure overlap, and a point touch is not an
overlap. `_overlap_defects` in `src/weedlib/figures.py`
still uses the area of `subtracted().simplified()`. That
area is the same triangle, about 0.003. The figure check
reports one overlap at the inner center, about
(4.950, 4.950). The same circles centered 6 apart report
no overlap. Centered 8 apart, the outlines cross and the
overlap stays. An external touch at distance 13 reports
none. `test_nesting_point_touch_and_shared_boundary_are_not_overlaps`
uses straight outlines, so it does not catch this.

Remedy: do not count a leftover the other fill contains as
exclusive area. The touching pair then has no exclusive
area, the outlines do not cross, and the pair is nesting.
Do not stop at `_outside_fill`. From about 7.001 through
7.07 the outlines cross while that helper is still false,
because the leftover sample sits in the outer fill. Those
pairs share area and must stay separate-figure overlaps.
A border cross has to keep the overlap. The circles
centered 8 apart already have a leftover point the outer
fill does not contain, so that overlap stays either way.
Not fixed in this pass.
