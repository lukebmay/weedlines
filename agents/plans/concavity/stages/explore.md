# Explore

## This stage

Compute candidate numbers for a cavern. More than one
number may be required. A threshold is what would later
decide whether a weed line seals that cavern. This
stage does not name the measure, does not add a
threshold control, and does not seal anything.

The kinds of number to probe are in [`../notes.md`](../notes.md).
They are examples. A task may time them and record
what they do to a large zig-zag tooth and a small one.
It does not adopt them as the measure.

The walk stays local and polynomial in the outline.
