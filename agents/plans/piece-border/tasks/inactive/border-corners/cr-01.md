## Summary

`piece_border`, `build_surrounds`, and `generate_weeds`
take an optional `corner_radius`. Omitted, `None`, zero,
or below, both paths stay the square rectangles, and both
clips stay on the rectangle. A larger radius is capped at
half the shorter side of the inner rectangle. Both
rectangles use that radius. The surround clip and the
solver clip follow the inner path. A rounded corner is
not clipped to the bounding box. The border width is not
the clip. The outer border is added after the clip. Open
surround ends stay open. Island-hop still runs.

A path is flattened before it is published only when that
path already has a curve. A square border has none, so a
cut that was already accepted with no radius still goes
through `_iter_line_segs` and the rectangle clip.

`src/weedlib/solvers/emit.py` is 764 lines. The new lines
are still the publish clip. The flatten lives in
`clip.py`. That is not a separate responsibility. No
split.
