## Summary

`generate_weeds` takes `inner_distance` and `border_width`.
Either one omitted leaves the mode path and its published
segments unchanged. When both are set, it calls
`piece_border` and clips the mode lines to that inner
rectangle. A published segment in `emit.py` uses the same
rectangle clip, and that clip is off when the lengths are
omitted. The two rectangles are appended after the clipped
lines, inner then outer, and they are not clipped.

The stored island-hop seal is an internal value. `_add_seg`
still writes the whole segment into the working path, so
ranking and clearance see the seal they saw before. The
clip is the copy `generate_weeds` returns and the segment
the host is shown. A returned or published line does not
pass outside the inner rectangle except the outer border.
