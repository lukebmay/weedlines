## Summary

`propose(check)` walks the defects the caller already has. It
does not run the check again and it does not change the
figures or the defect list. Each `Proposal` keeps that defect.
A dangling end and a separate overlap stay out of the list.
The remaining self-overlaps stay in the check's order. The
crossing comes from the border hit test. The figure check
files are not in this diff. The join and the weld are not
started.

The smooth-span path matches the host part when the gates
let it through. Two nodes sit on each side of the stack, the
stacked nodes are gone, and one cubic runs between the inner
nodes on the line through the outer nodes. The bowtie figure
8 is kept with no path. That keep happens because every node
of the bowtie sits inside the reach of the crossing, so the
quarter-gap test never runs.

## Defect

The figure check does not separate a smooth stack from a
corner stack. Every crossing is kind `self-overlap`. The
record is only that kind and a target. The host part names
the smooth-span fix, a corner stack, and a larger
self-overlap the person may keep. It names no angle and no
gap fraction.

`propose.py` uses two numbers the guide does not name as the
decision:

- `_SMOOTH_DOT` is the cosine of 20 degrees. A turn sharper
  than that is left out of the list, as a corner stack.
- `_STACK_FRACTION` is 0.25. A crossing whose pieces reach
  past a quarter of the gap to the next outside node is
  kept, so `path` is none.

20 degrees is not a number the guide names. A quarter of the
gap is not a number the guide names. A number the guide does
not name, used as the decision, is a defect.

The check's own kind cannot make this split. The only kind
on these crossings is `self-overlap`.

Remedy: stop and say the guide has no number. Do not use 20
degrees or a quarter of the gap as the decision. Do not
invent a replacement threshold.
