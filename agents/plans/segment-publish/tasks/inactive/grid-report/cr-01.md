Frame still publishes its rectangle once: four edges, each once.

The same segments match the old call order on that 80 by 60 keep, a 40 square, the letter O, and an inner 40 square.

The once-only timing has no test. `test_frame_and_grid_report_preview_segments` only checks that frame reports at least four segments and that grid reports more.
