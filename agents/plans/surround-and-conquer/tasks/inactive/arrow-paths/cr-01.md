# Arrow paths

No defects remain.

The change fits the task. `build_arrow_paths(kind, targets, bounds)`
returns one `ArrowPath` per target. The kind selects the origin and
the color. Color is not an argument. A target on that origin is a
short mark whose tip stays on the point. `figures.py` and
`solvers.py` are not part of this change.

The code fits that change. After `normalized()`, minimum y is the
top: dangling is top left, overlap is top right, and self-overlap
is bottom right. The shaft is one open line from that origin to
the middle of the head base. The head is a closed triangle whose
first point is the target. On the origin, the tail is offset along
the inward diagonal and the tip is still the target. The three
colors are the preview values.
