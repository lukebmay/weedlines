empty mode, and a whitespace-only mode store `Surround and Conquer`

## Fit

`agents/architecture/algorithms.md` says the host names Surround
and Conquer in the job when the person has not chosen a mode.
`weedlines.inx` has no mode parameter, so that launch omits
`--mode` and hits this default. `effect` builds one `defaults`
dict, then `write_job` and `run_weedlines_dialog` both receive
it. `write_job` copies that dict onto the job and does not
rewrite `mode`. `src/weedlib/solvers.py` is not in the diff. An
unknown mode there still strips, lowercases, and runs as `frame`.
