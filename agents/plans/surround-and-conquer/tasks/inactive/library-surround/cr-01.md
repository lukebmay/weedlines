# cr-01

## Result

One defect remains in this change. `pytest tests/test_surround.py`
passed (3 passed, no skips). Qt loaded. The tests do not load
Inkscape. `src/weedlib/solvers.py` is unchanged.

The distance-zero hole check does not hold for a curved subject.
The round-join check, the clearance check, and the gap check held
on the rectangle tests and on the extra cases below.

## Defect

`build_surrounds` at distance zero can return a loop that only
follows a hole. `_outer_boundaries` decides containment from the
vertices of `toSubpathPolygons`. Those are chords. A chord sits
inside a curve, so a hole vertex can be inside the subject and
outside the chord polygon. The hole's depth stays even, and the
loop is returned.

On this Qt binding (`enaml`), `addEllipse(-10, -10, 20, 20)`
flattens to 16 vertices. The deepest chord is at radius 9.802,
near 11.45 degrees. A point at radius 9.852 on that ray is inside
the ellipse (`contains` is true) and outside the chord polygon.
The odd-even subject is that ellipse plus a closed triangle:

- (9.656, 1.956)
- (9.256, 1.356)
- (8.856, 2.106)

`build_surrounds(subject, 0)` returns two paths. One is the
ellipse. The other is the triangle, about 0.8 by 0.75. The
triangle's box is inside the ellipse, so the outline does not
cross the curve.

The same miss happens with a smooth counter. An inner ellipse of
radius 6, shifted so the wall is 0.05 at that chord, also returns
two paths at distance zero. Two of its flattened vertices lie
outside the outer chord polygon.

A positive distance does not show this. Dilation simplifies the
path to lines first, and the hole above is omitted at distance
0.01. The rectangle hole in the suite never hits a chord gap, so
the suite stays green.

## Remedy

Nest the closed subpaths by a point the subpath contains, tested
with `QPainterPath.contains` on the other subpath. Use the center
of the bounding rect when that point is inside the subpath;
otherwise step in from an edge until `contains` is true. Count
only an outline that encloses more area. Keep an even depth. Do
not test chord vertices. Do not call `solvers.py`.

Add a distance-zero test whose hole vertex sits in that chord
gap. Expect one path, the outer curve, not the hole.

## Outside this change

`src/weedlib/figures.py` reports that triangle as a self-overlap.
The targets are about (9.603, 1.966) and (9.617, 1.897), where
the ellipse's chords cross the triangle. The real outlines do
not cross. `_self_overlap_defects` and `_borders_cross` both take
segments from `_border_segments`, which uses `toSubpathPolygons`.

The check is the self-overlap rule in
`agents/architecture/preflight.md`: a closed hole whose outline
does not cross the outer outline is not a self-overlap, and that
hole is the counter. The false crossing also makes
`_painted_parts` skip the counter, so the subject becomes the
solid disk. The offset ring with a 0.05 wall does the same.
A concentric ring does not. This was not introduced by the
surround change, and it is not fixed here.

## What held

Distance zero on a rectangle ring returns the outer rect only.
A piece in a rectangular hole stays its own cluster. At distance
7 the piece and the frame stay two surrounds at distance 3 and
become one at distance 4.

A square's surround is a round join. The miter corner stays off
the path. Dense samples stay at least about 1.9998 from the
square when the distance is 2, inside the test's 0.01. A circle
stays at least about 1.998 radially. An L outline's reflex
corner lands on the offset point (6, 6) and does not cut inside.

Edge gap and diagonal gap follow the real separation. Circle
gaps 3 and 3.9 at distance 2 return one surround. Gaps 4.1 and 5
return two. The suite's diagonal squares agree.

The returned path is one closed outer subpath. There is no
smoothing argument, no second pass, and no line from the surround
to the subject. The stroker is the one from `weedlib.qt`, with a
round join. Dilation scales a copy by 32 and sets the curve
threshold to 0.01. The caller's path is not mutated.
`build_surrounds` is exported from `weedlib`.

## Note

`_CLIP_SCALE` is the flatten scale, not a clip. The name is easy
to misread. It is not a failed check.
