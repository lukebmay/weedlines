## Summary

The change fits the defect. Closed subpaths nest by a point the
subpath contains, tested with `QPainterPath.contains`. Only a
larger outline adds depth. Even depth is still the outer
boundary. Area is the path integral of the elements, not the
chord polygon. The chord vertices are not used. `solvers.py` is
not called.

On this Qt a radius-10 ellipse still flattens to 16 vertices.
The deepest chord is at radius 9.802. The new test puts a
triangle vertex at radius 9.852, inside the ellipse and outside
that polygon, and expects the outer path only.

A hole that lies entirely in the gap is omitted too. Its
contained point sits at about radius 9.91, in the gap, and
`build_surrounds` returns the outer ellipse. An inner ellipse of
radius 6, shifted so the wall is 0.05 at that chord, returns one
path at distance zero and one at distance 1. The near point of
that hole is inside the curve and outside the chord polygon.

The caller's path is not mutated. Distance zero keeps the curve
elements. The integral for the radius-10 ellipse is about
314.25, against a chord-polygon area of about 306.22, and it
matches a fine sampling of the cubics.

## What held

Distance zero on a rectangle ring returns the outer rect only.
A piece in a rectangular hole is its own cluster. That piece's
gap is 7: two surrounds at distance 3, one at distance 4.

A centered island in a circular hole, gap 6, stays two surrounds
at distance 2.9 and becomes one at distance 3.1. An elliptical
hole with the island lying in that hole's chord gap returns the
outer rect and the island, not the hole. Four nested rectangles
return two outers. A clockwise hole is omitted. A circle sitting
in a C-shaped bay stays its own surround. A concentric hole,
including a 0.05 wall, is omitted.

A square's surround is a round join. The miter corner stays off
the path. An L at distance 2 stays at least about 1.9998 from
the outline, and no sample falls inside. Circle gaps 3.9 and 4.1
at distance 2 return one and two.

The returned path is one closed outer subpath. There is no
smoothing argument, no second pass, and no line from the
surround to the subject. The stroker is the one from
`weedlib.qt`, with a round join. Dilation scales a copy by 32
and sets the curve threshold to 0.01. `build_surrounds` is
exported from `weedlib`.

## Outside this change

`src/weedlib/figures.py` still reports the chord-gap triangle as
a self-overlap. That miss is already filed. It was not
introduced by this change, and it is not a defect of this pass.
