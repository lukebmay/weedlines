# cr-03

## Result

Two defects remain. `pytest tests/test_surround.py` passed (6
passed, no skips). Qt loaded. The tests do not load Inkscape.
`src/weedlib/solvers.py` is unchanged.

On the shapes the suite builds, one crossing pair is one
connector, the connector may come closer than the distance, the
end angles fall in range, and the L's inner corner is an arc of
that radius. A hole loop is not returned. Straight edges stay
straight. Those checks do not hold for a shallow crossing, for
unequal pieces, or for two inner corners that sit close together.
The suite has no test for those shapes.

## Defects

### A crossing is skipped unless both fillets fit

`_finish_outline` only smooths a clockwise turn steeper than
`_SHARP_TURN` (20 degrees). `_fit_fillet` then drops that corner
when the normal-offset centers miss by more than `_FILLET_ERR`
(0.05). `_snap_chords` draws a connector only when both ends of
the pair snapped to a fillet. Anything else keeps the raw corner
and draws no connector.

The outlines do cross in each case below. Distance is 2.

- Two 10 by 10 squares, edge gap 0.5. The pair is about
  (10.25, -1.984) and (10.25, 11.984). The union turn there is
  -13 degrees, under the 20 degree cutoff, so nothing is
  filleted. Neighboring vertices turn about 1.5 degrees. The
  finished kink is about 167 degrees. There is no connector.
- A 10 by 10 square beside a 10 by 4 square, edge gap 3.9. The
  turns are about -155 and -162 degrees. One fillet fits (center
  error about 0.019). The other stops at error 0.37. The chord
  is dropped. The failed corner stays a spike of about 18
  degrees, on a segment about 5 units long.
- A 10 by 10 square beside a circle of diameter 10 whose left
  side is at x = 12. Both center errors are about 0.17. Both
  corners stay. The finished angle is about 44 degrees. There
  is no connector.

The equal-square tests use gaps 3 and 3.9 and one diagonal pair.
Those fillets fit, the end angles land near 90, and the suite
stays green. The connector check has no test for a deep overlap
or for pieces of different shape.

### Close inner corners stay sharp

`_fit_fillet` will not set back more than `0.45` times the
distance along the outline to the next sharp corner, and not
more than `8` times the surround distance. A 90 degree turn of
radius equal to the distance needs a setback of that distance.
The cap refuses it when the next reflex corner is closer than
about `2.2` times the distance.

A U with a mouth of 6, walls down to y = 8, at distance 2, puts
the offset reflex corners at (14, 10) and (16, 10). Each turn is
-90 degrees and the outline gap between them is 2, so the cap
is 0.9. The same fillet with setback 2 has center error 0 and
stays outside the dilation. The result keeps both 90 degree
corners. A mouth of 8 does the same. A mouth of 10 is far enough
apart and is filleted. The L passes because it has one reflex
corner, so the cap is `8` times the distance, not `0.45` of a
short gap.

The inner-corner check has no test for two reflex corners.

## Remedy

Drive each connector from the crossing pair. Smooth both
crossings with a turn of radius at least the distance, including
when the raw turn is under 20 degrees and when the two
normal-offset centers miss by more than 0.05. Do not keep the
spike, and do not drop the connector because one end failed.
One pair is still one connector. Each end meets the smoothed
surround at an angle from 45 degrees through 135 degrees and
does not blend into it. The connector may come closer than the
distance. The rest may not.

When two reflex corners are too close for two separate
setbacks, do not leave them sharp. Replace that chain with one
turn whose radius is at least the distance, outside the
dilation. The 0.45 gap cap must not be what keeps a 90 degree
corner.

Add three cases at distance 2, each one path and no corner
sharper than the connector ends. Two squares at edge gap 0.5
expect one connector, with each end angle in range. A 10 by 10
beside a 10 by 4 at gap 3.9, and a square beside a circle,
expect the same. A U with a mouth of 6 expects no sharp inner
corner and a turn radius at least the distance. Do not call
`solvers.py`.

## What held

Equal 10 by 10 squares at edge gaps 3 and 3.9, and the diagonal
pair, return one surround. The crossing pair is one straight
connector. The stroke traverses that segment twice, so both ends
stay hard and the path stays one closed subpath. Deduped, that
is one connector, not a bridge at each crossing. The end angles
are about 89 to 96 degrees. The connector midpoint is closer
than the distance (about 1.5 at gap 3). Samples off that segment
stay at least about 1.9998 from the squares. A side point of the
first square still lies on the straight offset.

The L at distance 2 has no corner under about 176 degrees. The
arc by the reflex corner has radius 2.0. Clearance stays about
1.9998. A mild diagonal notch, a wide U, and a slit whose raw
turn is about -173 degrees also fillet.

Hole nesting is unchanged. A rectangular ring does not return
the hole. The island whose gap is 7 is two surrounds at distance
3 and one at distance 4, each a single subpath. The chord-gap
triangle at distance zero still returns only the outer ellipse.

A square's offset side is a straight segment. The round join
passes through the quarter-circle point, and the miter point
stays off the path. A diamond's outline does not bend its
straight edges. There is no smoothing argument, no second pass,
and no line from the surround to the subject. The stroker is the
one from `weedlib.qt`, round join. Dilation still scales a copy
by 32 and sets the curve threshold to 0.01. `build_surrounds`
is exported from `weedlib`. The caller's path is not mutated.

## Outside this change

`src/weedlib/figures.py` is not a defect of this change. The
self-overlap miss on the chord-gap triangle stays filed under
`plans/_bugs`.

A fillet joins the flattened outline with a step of about 7
degrees on the equal-square merge. The circumradius of that
triple is about 1.05, and the step is about 0.02. The suite
counts an angle over 150 degrees as smooth. This is not the
failed check.
