## Summary

The change fits the task. `subject_weed_regions(figures, swap)` takes
the figures from `classify_figures` and a swap flag. It returns
`SubjectWeed`: `subject` and `weed_area` are `QPainterPath`s, and
`bounds` is the axis-aligned box of those figures. It does not
classify figures again. `src/weedlib/solvers.py` is unchanged.

Painted interiors, from the same `_painted_parts` the figure check
uses, start as subject. `none` is not paint. A color or `other`
is. When no figure has paint, every interior is subject. Weed area
is that box minus the subject, so gaps and holes are inside it and
the sheet outside the box is in neither path. Swap exchanges the
two paths. A later call with the flag false restores the first
assignment. The bounds stay put. Defects on the figure check are
not added or removed.

The code matches that. The two preview cases hold: filled letters
inside a stroke-only rectangle, and a filled rectangle whose holes
are the letters.

subject. The stroke-only rectangle does not punch the painted
