## Defects

   shorter than 1 or the samples miss it. `_boundary_hidden`
   skips `length < 1` and samples an edge only at
   `k / ceil(length)`. A side of height 1 is stored as length
   `0.9999999999999998`, so the cutoff drops it. On a longer
   edge the only samples can sit on the facing part, and the
   free end is never tested. The hull is kept.

   Rectangles `(0, 0, 30, 10)` and `(0, 10.05, 29.2, 0.9)`,
   distance 0, join gap 0.1, should be area 327.74, with

   hull, area 3.575. `(1.5, 1)` is 0.362 inside, and `(1.5, 1.2)`
   is inside. It is 0.5 from the upper square and 0.2 from the
   wide one.

   At distance 2 the same cutoff fills the pocket. Rectangles
   `(0, 0, 30, 10)` and `(0, 15, 29.2, 0.5)`, join gap 1.
   `(29.6, 12)` is the wide top's free end. It is 2.11 inside
   the outline. `(29.6, 12.5)` is inside the join and outside
   each separate surround: 2.5 from the wide rectangle and 2.53

   because that free run is long enough to be sampled.

   Remedy: a straight side counts even when it is shorter than
   1. Do not use a length cutoff that a float of 1 fails. An
   arc chord is the edge whose ends both turn with the arc and
   which is not colinear with a neighbor. On a straight side,
   test a point that does not face the gap, including the free
   end, not only the midpoint or a 1-unit grid. If that point
   lies inside the tangent, bridge the full facing overlap.
   `(29.6, 10)` and `(29.6, 12)` stay on the outline, within
   0.01. `(29.6, 10.3)` and `(29.6, 12.5)` stay outside.
