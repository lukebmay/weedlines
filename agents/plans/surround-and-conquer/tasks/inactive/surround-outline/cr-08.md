   new edge can pass through the subject between those probes.
   `_bridge_clears` in `src/weedlib/surround.py` samples 0.25,
   0.5, and 0.75 only. At distance 0 the limit is 0, so a probe
   just outside the subject passes. At a positive distance a probe
   passes when it is at least the distance from the subject, and
   the open span between probes can still hold a finger.

   The frame is `(-15, -15, 55, 45)` with hole `(-5, -5, 35, 25)`.

   The same hole shows at distance 2 when the bridge is longer
