## Summary

The change fits the defect. A solid figure is no longer an
odd-even fill of `figure.path`. In `refresh_preview` the overlay
is `_painted_parts` on `split_painter_path(figure.path)`,
intersected with the current subject, then filled in that
figure's `#rrggbb`. The dialog calls `_painted_parts`. It does
not copy the interior. A subject with no paint, or with other
paint, still has no solid overlay.

The intersection is what keeps a swap from leaving the solid in
place. On the swapped subject the part is empty, so the former
subject, including the lens, is weed area. The next press
restores the fill.

The rest of the preview is the previous pass. The window paints
the library subject and weed area, and the paths from
`build_arrow_paths`. Swap is one button, "Swap subject and weed
area". The four initial colors are the Paint table. Weed lines
use the weed-line color. Start stays disabled while the check
reports a defect. There is no return line. Solvers were not
edited.
