## Defects

The dropdown offers `yd`, `q`, and `km`. Inkscape's linear
display units are `px`, `in`, `ft`, `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `pt`,
and `pc`. Percent and em are correctly absent.

`km` does not show a normal length. On a one-millimeter
document the join gap, a padding of 4, and the five-percent
distance all show 0. The stored join is still 6.35 until the
spin is edited. One step is 0.1 km, and the library then
receives 100000 user units, not 6.35. The surround distance
on that same start stayed 2.5 because that spin was not
stepped.

Remedy: limit the dropdown to those eight units. Leave `q`,
`yd`, and `km` in `weedlines_svg._INCHES_PER_UNIT` for SVG
lengths. Do not keep `km` by adding decimals. A step of 0.1
km is still 100000 millimeters.

spins show one display unit. The library and the solvers
still receive document user units. Switching converts the
digits and the ranges. A padding of 200 mm becomes about
755.9055 px and is not clamped to 200, then about 7.874 in.

scale receives 0.635, not 6.35. Surround distance stays five

`weedlines_svg._INCHES_PER_UNIT`. The SVG is not read again.
`dialog.display_unit` stays the job's unit. The combo is the
live display. A job unit of em keeps `dialog.display_unit`
as em, the combo starts on px, the spin shows 24, and the

Surround and timing tests that still mean a millimeter
document pass that scale. The cut tests in
`tests/test_window_gate.py` were not edited.
`tests/test_window_preview.py` was not edited.
