## Defects

None. The dropdown is Inkscape's eight linear units: `px`,
`in`, `ft`, `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `pt`, and `pc`. `yd`, `q`, and
`km` are not choices. They stay in
`weedlines_svg._INCHES_PER_UNIT`. Decimals stay 4, so
kilometers were not kept by showing more places. Percent and
em are absent.

A job unit of `km` keeps `dialog.display_unit` and the combo
starts on px. That start is not a defect. On a one-millimeter
document the join spin shows 24. Start sends 6.35 user units,
not 24 and not 0. The same fallback covers `yd`, `q`,
percent, and em.

## Summary

The display-length change fits the task and the boundary.
The dialog uses the job's display unit and
`user_unit_scale`. It does not read the SVG. The inch table
is the conversion constant.

Every length spin shows one display unit. The unit is the
suffix. Labels are "Padding", "Grid spacing",
"Collar (island-hop)", and "Join gap". Switching converts
the digits and the ranges. A padding of 200 mm becomes about
755.9055 px and is not clamped to 200. Spacing of 25 mm
becomes about 0.984 in, below 1.

The library and the solvers still receive document user
units. The join gap starts at a quarter inch under the
document scale. A millimeter document shows 6.35 and the
library receives 6.35. With no scale the library receives
24. A centimeter scale receives 0.635. Surround distance
stays five percent of the smaller side. On the 80 by 50
sample that is 2.5 user units.

Padding, spacing, and the collar stay user-unit counts. A
padding of 4 mm shows about 0.1575 in, and the solver
receives 4. Setting that inch spin to 1 sends 25.4. Setting
spacing to 2 sends 50.8. Switching back to mm shows those
counts. `generate_weeds` is called with those user-unit
counts.

Surround and timing tests that still mean a millimeter
document pass that scale. `tests/test_window_gate.py` and
`tests/test_window_preview.py` were not edited.
