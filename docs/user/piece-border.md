# See the piece border

The piece border is two rectangles around the piece. The piece
is the subject you are keeping.

## Open the window

- [ ] In Inkscape, choose **Extensions → Weedlines → 1 Auto
  generate weedlines**. The window opens.

## Read the line

- [ ] Read the line under the preview. When it names problems,
  the two rectangles are not drawn. Follow
  [Place correction arrows](correction-arrows.md). When that
  line is empty, continue.

## Look without starting

- [ ] Leave **Start** unpressed. The two rectangles are drawn
  in the **Weed line color** without choosing **Start**. They
  are not the surround, and they are not the padding of
  frame, grid, or island-hop.

## Set the border

- [ ] Find **Border Padding**. It is the gap from the piece
  out to the inner rectangle. It starts at one eighth of
  an inch.
- [ ] Find **Border Width**. It is the width of the strip
  out to the outer rectangle. It starts at one eighth of
  an inch.
- [ ] Leave **Border Rounded Corners** off. The corners are
  square, and **Border Corner Radius** is hidden.
- [ ] Turn **Border Rounded Corners** on. **Border Corner
  Radius** appears. Until you set it, it is one quarter of
  an inch. It cannot go below zero, and it cannot pass
  half the shorter side of the inner rectangle.

## Swap the subject

- [ ] Choose **Swap subject and weed area**. The two rectangles
  are drawn again for the new subject.
