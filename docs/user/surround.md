# Start the surround

The window opens on Surround and Conquer. It waits. No surround
is drawn until you start that step.

## Open the window

- [ ] In Inkscape, choose **Extensions → Weedlines → Weedlines
  from selection…**. The window opens on **Surround and
  Conquer**.

## Read the line

- [ ] Read the line under the preview. When it names problems,
  no surround is shown and **Start Algorithm** stays off. Follow
  [Place correction arrows](correction-arrows.md). When that
  line is empty, continue.

## Wait

- [ ] Leave **Start Algorithm** unpressed. The preview shows the
  subject and the weed area, and it shows no surround. Setting
  either length below does not draw one.

## Set the two lengths

- [ ] Find **Surround distance**. It is on the form only while
  the mode is Surround and Conquer. Until you set a number, it
  is five percent of the shorter side of the box around the
  design. The window keeps your number while it stays open.
- [ ] Change the number to set the clearance between the subject
  and the surround. The subject is the region you are keeping.
  The number cannot go below zero. Zero is the outer boundary
  of the subject. It is a length in the document's units.
- [ ] Find **Join gap**. It is on the form only in this mode.
  Until you set it, it is a quarter of an inch under the
  document scale, shown in the unit the window is using. It
  cannot go below zero. Zero joins only surrounds that meet or
  overlap. A larger number also joins surrounds that stay at
  most that far apart.

Padding, grid spacing, the collar, and the output layer do not
change the surround.

## Start

- [ ] Choose **Start Algorithm**. The surround is drawn in the
  **Weed line color**, on top of the checkerboard. That does
  not write a result.
- [ ] Change **Surround distance** or **Join gap**. The
  surround follows the new length.
- [ ] Choose **Swap subject and weed area**. The surround follows
  the swapped regions.
- [ ] Change **Weed line color**. The lines are repainted in that
  color. They stay.
- [ ] Change to another mode. The lines clear and your numbers
  stay. Choose **Surround and Conquer** again. The surround is
  drawn again.

## Change the surround

- [ ] Choose **Change surround**. The lines clear. Your numbers
  stay. No surround is drawn until you choose **Start
  Algorithm** again.
- [ ] Leave this mode and come back. The numbers are still
  there. No surround is drawn until the next **Start
  Algorithm**.
